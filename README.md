# ArchiveVault Support

Public support, troubleshooting, and privacy information for **ArchiveVault — Your Personal Media Archive**.

Visit the support site: <https://sur5an.github.io/ArchiveMark-Support/>

## Get support

Open a [support request](https://github.com/sur5an/ArchiveMark-Support/issues/new?template=support-request.md). Include your device model, operating-system version, ArchiveVault version, and the steps that led to the problem. Never post personal media or backup records.

## Privacy summary

ArchiveVault does not require an account and does not send media, files, credentials, or usage activity to the developer. It reads only the libraries and folders selected by the user and writes copies and verification records directly to the selected local, external, Files-provider, or SMB destination. The full privacy policy is published on the support site.
