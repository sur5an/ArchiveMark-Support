Quick start

Your first backup

ArchiveVault performs a one-way backup. It never deletes originals from your phone or tablet.

  1. 1
    Connect your library

    Allow access to the photos and videos you want ArchiveVault to see.

  2. 2
    Choose a destination

    Select an external drive, a local Files folder, or a compatible SMB network share.

  3. 3
    Review and back up

    ArchiveVault shows the items waiting, copies them, and verifies every completed file.

  4. 4
    Reconnect when needed

    Refresh the library later to find new items. Verified files are not copied again.

Common questions

Troubleshooting

Why is the backup button disabled?+

Reconnect the saved drive, folder, or SMB share, then refresh the library. ArchiveVault keeps backup disabled while the destination is unavailable.

What if manifest.csv is deleted?+

ArchiveVault can rebuild verified records from its local catalogue when the destination files still match. Your media files remain the source of truth.

Why can a Live Photo count as two files?+

On iPhone, a Live Photo contains an image and a short video. ArchiveVault counts one library item but verifies both original files.

What does Integrity Check do?+

It reads backed-up files, confirms their contents, and recreates missing or damaged copies when the original is available.

Can a backup continue while the device is locked?+

ArchiveVault uses the background time available on your device. For very large backups, keep the app open and the device powered; reopening safely reconciles completed files.

Privacy

Your archive stays yours.

ArchiveVault does not require an account, collect analytics, track you, or upload your media to an ArchiveVault cloud service.

Photo library

Access is used to show backup status and read originals selected for backup.

Files and destinations

ArchiveVault accesses only the source folders and local, external, or SMB destination you choose.

SMB credentials

If you configure an SMB share, its credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain on your device and used only to connect to that share.

Backup records

Technical records such as file names, sizes, dates, checksums, device identifiers, and backup results are kept on your device and in the selected archive destination.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled on your device when new media may be waiting for backup.

Retention and deletion

ArchiveVault keeps local settings and records until you remove them or delete the app. Backup files and records remain at your destination until you delete them.

Your choices

You can limit Photos access, remove selected Files folders, change the destination, disable reminders, and remove saved SMB credentials by deleting that destination.

Security

ArchiveVault verifies copied files with SHA-256. Network security also depends on your SMB server and the connection options it supports.

Third-party software

SMB support uses AMSMB2 under the MIT License and libsmb2 under the LGPL-2.1-or-later license.

Children and changes

ArchiveVault is not directed to children and knowingly collects no personal data. Material privacy changes will be posted on this page.
